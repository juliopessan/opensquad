# Squad Memory — Sustainable Packaging LinkedIn

## Overview
Multi-agent squad para criar conteúdo LinkedIn de alta qualidade sobre sustainable packaging, com foco em brand recognition e engagement com decision makers.

## Key Learnings & Context

### Company Profile
- **Name:** Decpack
- **Sector:** Sustainable Packaging & Design
- **Differentiators:** Design inteligente, personalização, agilidade, time de designers
- **Target Audience:** Delivery, varejo, indústria, e-commerce
- **Brand Voice:** Profissional, sofisticado, focado em valor e resultados

### Research Insights Applied

#### LinkedIn Content Marketing
- Hook placement critical: optimize first 210-235 characters for visibility
- Optimal post length: 1,300-1,600 characters for dwell time
- Carousel format: 5x more clicks, 150% engagement lift
- Personal voices outperform company pages 8x
- Comments signal quality more than likes

#### Sustainable Packaging Psychology
- Trust is the primary driver (transparency over vague claims)
- Cost-benefit focus: 40% weight reduction = $X savings
- Greenwashing kills faster than anything
- Gen Z willing to pay 49% premium (vs 37% older cohorts)

#### B2B Decision Making
- Decision units are 12+ people (need role-specific messaging)
- Emotions + data win (validate pain first, then shift to proof)
- Role-specific CTAs: ROI/case studies (exec), implementation (ops), specs (procurement)
- Trust signals: testimonials, case studies, certifications, referrals

#### Brand Positioning
- Packaging is differentiation tool, not commodity
- Sustainability is now expected, not nice-to-have
- Authority comes from content (guides, benchmarks, trends)
- Repeatable content pillars: Cost Savings, Compliance, Innovation, Customer Stories

### Content Angles That Work
- Why sustainability cuts costs (ROI-focused)
- Real customer testimonials on implementation
- Industry-specific wins
- Myth-busting: "Sustainable packaging doesn't cost more long-term"
- Role-specific proof: case studies, risk mitigation, ESG alignment

### Pipeline Strategy (Economic Mode)
- Simple 3-agent pipeline: Strategist → Creator → Reviewer
- No A/B variants or optimization passes
- Lightweight review (fast approval)
- Single format per piece (post OR article, not both simultaneously)

## Performance Metrics to Track
- Engagement rate (comments > likes)
- Click-through rate on CTAs
- Lead generation from posts
- Audience growth (decision makers followed)
- Share rate of articles

4. Scale successful formats and angles
